# MaxWear Apparel Co. — Official Support Policy Handbook

## 1. Standard Returns & Exchanges (`POL-RET-01`)
- **Return Window:** 30 calendar days from the confirmed delivery date.
- **Condition Requirements:** Items must be unworn, unwashed, free of stains/odors, and have all original hang tags attached.
- **Refund Timeline:** Processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of warehouse inspection.

## 2. Clearance & Final Sale Policy (`POL-RET-02`)
- **Rule:** Any product marked `Final Sale` or `Clearance` (`final_sale: true`) is **strictly non-returnable, non-refundable, and ineligible for exchange under any circumstances**.
- **Scope:** Applies storewide with **no exceptions permitted for any customer tier**.

## 3. VIP Gold Loyalty Tier Perks (`POL-VIP-01`)
- **Rule:** Customers holding `VIP_GOLD` status enjoy an extended **60-day hassle-free return guarantee on ALL items purchased**, explicitly **including clearance and Final Sale items**, plus free standard and return shipping.

> **IMPORTANT GOVERNANCE NOTE (`POL-ESC-01`):**  
> There is an unresolved policy conflict (`CONF-VIP-FINALSALE`) between `POL-RET-02` (Final Sale has zero exceptions) and `POL-VIP-01` (VIP Gold covers Final Sale items). Support agents **must never guess or unilaterally approve/reject** a Final Sale return for a `VIP_GOLD` customer—always escalate to a Human Supervisor via `escalate_to_human`.

## 4. Order Cancellations (`POL-CAN-01`)
- **Eligible State:** Orders in `processing` state can be cancelled immediately prior to shipment.
- **Ineligible States:** Orders in `shipped` or `delivered` state cannot be cancelled in transit; customers must wait for delivery and initiate a return if eligible.

## 5. Shipping & Fulfillment (`POL-SHP-01`)
- **Standard Shipping:** 3–5 business days (\$7 flat rate; FREE on orders over \$100 or for `VIP_GOLD` members).
- **Express Shipping:** 1–2 business days (\$18 flat rate).
