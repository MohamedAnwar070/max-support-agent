# UrbanThread — Official Customer Support Policy Handbook

## 1. Standard 7-Day Return & Refund Policy (`POL-RET-01`)
- **Return Window:** **7 calendar days** from the confirmed delivery date.
- **Condition Requirements:** Apparel must be unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached.
- **Mock Refund Processing:** Approved returns issue a mock refund with a unique `request_key` (`REQ-...`) and refund reference (`REF-...`), credited back to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.

## 2. Clearance & Final Sale Policy (`POL-RET-02`)
- **Rule:** Any product marked `Final Sale` or `Clearance` (`final_sale: true`) is **strictly non-returnable, non-refundable, and ineligible for exchange under any circumstances**.
- **Scope:** Applies storewide with **no exceptions permitted for any customer tier**.

## 3. VIP Gold Loyalty Tier Perks (`POL-VIP-01`)
- **Rule:** Customers holding `VIP_GOLD` status receive an extended **14-day return and refund guarantee on ALL items purchased**, explicitly **including clearance and Final Sale items**, plus complimentary shipping.

> **GOVERNANCE ESCALATION RULE (`POL-ESC-01`):**  
> Policy `POL-RET-02` (Final Sale has zero exceptions) and Policy `POL-VIP-01` (VIP Gold covers Final Sale items) directly conflict (`CONF-VIP-FINALSALE`). Support agents **must escalate without guessing** whenever a `VIP_GOLD` customer requests a return or refund on a Final Sale item.

## 4. Order Cancellations (`POL-CAN-01`)
- **Eligible State:** Orders in `processing` state can be cancelled immediately prior to shipment for a full mock refund.
- **Ineligible States:** Orders in `shipped` or `delivered` state cannot be cancelled in transit.

## 5. Shipping & Fulfillment (`POL-SHP-01`)
- **Standard Shipping:** 3–5 business days (\$6 flat rate; FREE on orders over \$75 or for `VIP_GOLD` members).
- **Express Shipping:** 1–2 business days (\$15 flat rate).
