# Customer-Support Agent for an Apparel Company — Candidate Task

**Build from scratch; do not reuse existing projects or previous work. You may use any AI tools.**

1. **Write a short PRD:** customer needs, scope, assumptions and success criteria.

2. **Create fictional data:** products, policies, customers and orders. No real customer data or transactions.

3. **Build a working agent:** answer a variety of customer questions (using your own judgment) to mock real life scenarios. Clarify or escalate to human beings when information is missing or an action is unsafe.

4. **Test six failure types:** missing information, conflicting policies, unauthorised order access, prompt injection, tool failure and duplicate requests. Show expected versus actual results.

5. **Write repeatable agent evals:** include pass/fail criteria, run instructions and results.

6. **Within one hour, submit a ZIP to hiring@gomaxify.ai containing the PRD, data, code, tests, evals and setup README. Exclude credentials.
